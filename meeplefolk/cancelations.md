# Booking Cancelation Policy

We are sorry to say that we do not offer refunds for our ticketed events. This is due to the fees that are involved with bookings.

If you need to cancel a session, we are happy to move your booking to another available week providing that we have reasonable notice for a genuine reason.

Where possible, please try to let us know 24 hours in advance. We understand however that things can change, so under a fair use basis, we will often be happy to rebook people with less than 24 hours notice. 

Any questions, please let us know.

## Contacting us

The best way to cancel is by using the form. You can also reach out to us ether in the Aftergame App, Discord, or by email. Links to all three can be found below in the "Contact and socials" section.
