# Welcome, to Ravenswood Bluff!

On a dark and stormy night, a flash of lighning illuminates the town square before you.
You see a body, motionless at the base of the clocktower, a red shimmering on the cobblestone around them.
As you approach,you have a feeling you are not alone, a feeling of being watched. You discover that the body is (or was) the towns storyteller.
As you look apon them, you see the nature of their death is something unfathomable, you believe it to be the work of a demon!


## What is Clocktower?

Blood on the Clocktower is a social deduction game for 5-20 players.
Everyone playing will have their own unique ability, and no two games are ever the same.
At the start of the game, you draw a token out of the bag. If blue, you are on the good team, and if red, you are on the evil team.
The good team have abilities that will help them solve and win the game. Abilities like learning if your living
neighbours are evil, protecting someone from death, or abilities that alow you to build trust with others. The good team win the game if they find execute the demon.

The evil team know eachother, but will be small in number. Usually 2-4 people. They also have abilities that will help their team to victory.
Abilities that kill, poison, spy, or give the evil team a second lifeline after the original demon dies.
If the evilteam manage to kill everyone in town, until just two remain, then the evil team win!

The game is a living, breathing puzzle. Can you work out who is lying? What information works and what doesn't? Will the game be solved before time, runs, out...

## New to Clocktower?

We run Beginner Friendly sessions on the first Tuesday of the month. For new players, this session is free!
You dont need to know anything about the game before coming along. The game storyteller will explain the basics, and will be on hand to help you with any questions. Our experienced players will also gladly help explain anything throughout the night.
