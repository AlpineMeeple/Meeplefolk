# Social Board Game sessions

**Thursday evenings, and the 4th Sunday of the month.**  
*Located at The Wote Street Club in Basingstoke.*

Board Games are a fantastic way to get together and share time with others at the table.

![]({{ '/assets/images/AlpineMeeple.png' | relative_url }}){: .text-meeple .right-meeple} ![]({{ '/assets/images/blue-meeple.png' | relative_url }}){: .text-meeple .right-meeple} We have a variety of games in our collection with ranging levels of complexity. **Thursday Evenings - Free**

Every Thursday from 18:00-22:00 we have a couple of tables within the club open for Board Games. We don't have exclusive use of the venue on these nights and as such, the event is free! The club can sometimes become a bit noisy but we find its often OK for our sessions.

If noise is a concern, look at our Sunday session where we pay for exclusive use.
